# Bless: 30 seconds + 1 round per level

Status: decided by Christopher on 2026-10-23; a part of component 340 (Chris
Revisions). Builds on the [first batch](2026-10-23-aid-luck-chaotic-commands.md),
[Prayer and Recitation](2026-10-23-prayer-recitation.md) and the
[October 11 review](2026-10-11-general-spell-balance-design.md).

## Decision

Christopher: "Ja, Bless auf 30 s + 1 Runde pro Stufe umstellen, als weiterer
Teil von Komponente 340."

| | Unmodded | Spell Revisions | Chris Revisions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Duration | 6 rounds (36 s), fixed | 5 turns (300 s), fixed | 30 s + 1 round per caster level |
| Level 1 / 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 | 36 s | 300 s | 36 / 60 / 90 / 120 / 150 s |

From level 20 on, Bless stays at 150 seconds: Christopher accepted the proposal
as "36–150 s", and like most level tables in the game the new abilities end at
level 20. The bonuses, range and area are unchanged.

His goal for Bless was that it stays worth memorizing at higher levels. The
duration now grows with the caster instead of SR's fixed five turns, which let
one cast before a fight last through several. Bless keeps stacking with Chant,
Prayer and Recitation ([decision](2026-10-23-prayer-recitation.md#decision)).

Open: Spell Revisions excludes druids from Bless; unmodded druids can cast it.
Whether druids get it back, as with Chaotic Commands, is Christopher's decision
and not part of this change.

## Component design

1. Resolve `CLERIC_BLESS` through SPELL.IDS. Without it, the part is skipped.
2. The family is the spell, its exact copies (byte-identical abilities and
   effects, as for Aid and Luck) and the copies SCS's map names
   `CLERIC_BLESS_PREBUFF`. The map rule applies to every family part: a
   prebuff copy is the same spell for SCS's casters. SCS's list has no Aid or
   Luck today, so only Bless gains a member from it.
3. Each member gets one ability per caster level from 1 to 20. Each copies the
   ability the game used at that level: the one with the highest level not
   above it, or the lowest one below the first level. Range, casting time,
   projectile and the other ability fields stay.
4. In each new ability, every timed effect (timing 0, 3 or 4) that lasted
   exactly as long as its Bless bonus (opcode 130, timing 0) lasts
   `30 + 6 × level` seconds: the bonus, the spell state, the icon and the end
   sound. Other durations stay, such as SR's 1-second and the native 2-second
   visual.
5. Abilities for levels above 20, which neither shape has, stay with the
   level-20 duration.
6. An ability whose Bless bonuses have different durations, or that has no timed
   Bless bonus, leaves Bless unchanged with a warning. A dry run checks every
   member first, SCS's prebuff copy included: it changes with the spell or not
   at all.
7. Each ability gets its own copy of its effects; casting effects stay first. A
   second run is byte-identical.
8. Descriptions: the `Duration:` line becomes "Duration: 5 rounds + 1
   round/level (maximum 25 rounds)". Other wording stays, with a warning. Items
   that show the old text show the new one; CEBG has none.

## Consequences

- A level-1 cleric's Bless lasts as long as unmodded. SR's five turns are gone
  at every level, so Bless is no longer a buff cast well before a fight.
- Spells and items that cast Bless at a fixed level inherit that level's
  duration. Glimmer of Hope's level-1 Bless lasts 36 seconds instead of 300.
- SCS's AI casts Bless as before; its Bless simply ends sooner.
- Bless effects already running in a save keep their old duration until they
  expire.

## Verification

- `tests/test_chris_revisions.py` (`BlessTests`, `CombinedTests` and a Luck
  prebuff test): real WeiDU on synthetic games shaped like the files above. It
  covers both shapes, an exact copy, a near copy (untouched), SCS's prebuff copy
  with its map, a map entry without a file, a relocated Bless with its own
  levels 3, 10 and 25 and a casting effect, unchanged ability fields and header,
  the descriptions and an item showing the text, conflicting or missing
  bonuses (left unchanged with a warning), untranslated text, a missing symbol,
  a second run (no change) and uninstall. The map file itself is never written.
- On 2026-10-23 component 340 was installed on scratch copies of the CEBG
  resources (with SCS's map) and of the 2.6.6.0 reference install, each with its
  `dialog.tlk`. `SPPR101` and `DWSP101` went from one to 20 abilities lasting
  36 to 150 seconds, with every other effect and ability field unchanged; the
  header changed only in the description, ability count and effect offset. The
  description was recognized, the other opcode-130 spells and Glimmer of Hope
  stayed unchanged, and uninstalling restored every file. With the BIFF
  originals only `SPPR101` changed, from 36 seconds fixed to 36–150.

## Limitations

- Not yet checked in a running game. A lab check should cast Bless at levels 1,
  10 and 20 and watch the icon and end sound.
- Descriptions are patched in English only.
- Druid access is unchanged until Christopher decides.

# v0.8.10 — Flail of the Ages without Spell Revisions

Flail of the Ages (302) gives the flail's Slow a saving throw and lets the +5 version work with
Haste. This release makes it install in games without Spell Revisions and makes the +5 keep its
Haste promise for potions and rings too.

- **Installs without Spell Revisions.** With SCS's own Haste and Slow system ("Haste and Slow
  effects cancel", part of SCS's spell tweaks when Spell Revisions is not installed), the
  component stopped with "BLUN14.SPL mixes Slow with other effects". It now installs there.
  A target that makes the save is not slowed, keeps its Haste and sees no "Slowed" message; a
  hasted target that fails it loses Haste instead of being slowed, as SCS intends.
- **The +5 lets Potion of Haste and Haste rings work**, as well as Haste and Improved Haste,
  with and without Spell Revisions. Before, the +5 still blocked them: without Spell Revisions
  because the potion's Haste sits in a spell it casts, with Spell Revisions because the +5's
  "cannot be hasted" state stayed. The +5 keeps every other protection: Free Action, and
  immunity to Slow, Hold, Web and Entangle.
- **The saves are unchanged**: vs. Breath at -2 for the +3, -4 for both +4 versions and -6 for
  the +5. Proc chance, duration, damage and the lower tiers stay as they were.

If another mod mixes damage or other effects into the flail's Slow helper, or into an innate
Haste, the installer still stops (helper) or keeps the immunity (innate Haste) and says why.

Requirements are unchanged: BG2:EE or EET, all four flails present; no EEex.

Automated checks: 840 tests, OK, 24 skipped (private dragon captures) with WeiDU 249. The new
ones run WeiDU on synthetic games:
- SCS's layout without Spell Revisions: the four flails with their helpers, Haste, Improved
  Haste, Slow and the innate Haste with the spells they cast;
- Spell Revisions' and the vanilla layouts as before;
- helpers with damage, an extra save, a penalty longer than the Slow or an area projectile;
- an innate Haste with damage or missing;
- an unconfirmed spell state;
- a second install and uninstalling.

Dry runs on copies of real resources, with no game folder written:
- **A CEBG test install without Spell Revisions:** the four flails got the save and nothing
  else changed on hit. The +5 lost exactly its seven Haste blockers (53 → 46 equipped effects).
  Uninstalling restored every file byte for byte.
- **Spell Revisions data:** the same seven blockers.
- **Vanilla data:** as before.

Not tested: the flail in the running game (no in-game test yet), a localized game, and other
item or spell overhauls of the flail. The component has not been accepted in real gameplay in
this layout.

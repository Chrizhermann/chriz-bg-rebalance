# v0.8.2 — Flail of the Ages works with more mods

- **Flail of the Ages (302):** the Slow saving throw and the Haste-compatible
  +5 now also work when other mods change the flails: extra abilities or
  effects, reordered or shared effect records, linked helper spells, and
  different description wording. The balance is unchanged: save vs. Breath at
  -2 (+3), -4 (+4) and -6 (+5); damage, proc chance and Slow duration stay.
- Helpers that mix Slow with other effects are not silently changed; the
  install stops with the reason instead.
- The +5 still allows Haste from real Haste spells, with or without Spell
  Revisions, and keeps its other protections.

No other component changed. Install as before; the mod does not edit saves.

Automated checks: 565 tests with WeiDU 249 (24 skipped that need private
captures), including the four Flail suites, real vanilla and Spell Revisions
Haste spell shapes and rollback/uninstall checks. They do not replace an
in-game check.

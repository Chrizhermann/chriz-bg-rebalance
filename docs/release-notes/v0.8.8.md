# v0.8.8 — Druid weapons and Alaghor of Clangeddin weapons

Two new components give druids, fighter/druids and Yeslick's Alaghor of
Clangeddin kit more weapons, changing as little as possible.

- **Druid weapons (410):** druids and every druid kit may use axes and short
  bows and become Proficient in them (one pip); fighter/druids may also use
  long bows and specialize (two pips) in all three. A higher limit set by
  another mod stays. With Artisan's Kitpack the component adds these class
  grants to the Kitpack's EEex weapon permissions (`C0PR#CL`); without it, it
  removes the druid and fighter/druid restrictions from the matching items.
  The English druid and fighter/druid descriptions name the new weapons.
- **Alaghor of Clangeddin weapons (411):** Yeslick NPC's Alaghor of Clangeddin
  kit trains and uses the cleric weapons and axes up to the fighter/cleric
  limits, at most two pips; the thief weapons its column allowed drop out, and
  pips already spent stay. The engine reads a fighter/cleric's weapon limits
  from the fighter/cleric column even with a cleric kit, so an EEex hook makes
  it read the kit's own column for fighter/clerics with this kit; everyone
  else stays native. The English kit description names the axes.

Requirements: BG2:EE or EET. 410 works with or without Artisan's Kitpack and
Spell Revisions. 411 needs Yeslick NPC, Artisan's Kitpack with its EEex weapon
permissions, and EEex. Its installer refuses an executable in which the hooked
instructions do not occur exactly once. At every game start the hook checks
them again; if another mod changed them, the game stays native, and one line
in InfinityLoader's log says why; there is never a message in the game.
Install both after Artisan's Kitpack (and its "Update EEex-based Weapon
Usability Changes" tweak, if used) and after every mod that adds druid kits or
changes WEAPPROF; 411 also after Yeslick NPC. With the Kitpack, the party
members of a saved game get the new permissions at their next level-up at the
latest. No other component changed.

Automated checks: 820 tests, OK, 26 skipped (private captures) with WeiDU 249.
Among them, 25 checks of 410 and 411 run WeiDU on synthetic games, and 8 run
the hook's Lua against a simulated EEex. Labs on BG2:EE 2.7.3.0 with EEex
1.3.1, installed with WeiDU:

- 410, in three labs (Artisan's Kitpack chriz.3 with Spell Revisions and the
  Kitpack's update tweak, in CEBG's order; chriz.4-rc2 without Spell
  Revisions, in another order; no Kitpack): a druid, a Beastfriend, a
  Hivemaster and a fighter/druid could equip exactly the weapons the rules
  allow, and the level-up screen offered the new limits. That held after
  joining, a level-up and a save and reload. Uninstalling restored
  `override`.
- 411, in CEBG's order (Yeslick NPC, Spell Revisions, Artisan's Kitpack
  chriz.4 with the update tweak, 410, 411): the hook switched on. Yeslick and
  two fighter/clerics with the kit were offered the axe and the cleric weapons
  up to two pips and no thief weapon; one spent a free pip on the axe, and all
  three equipped it. A fighter/cleric without the kit got no axe. That held
  after a save and reload. Uninstalling restored `override`.

Not lab-tested: 411 on EEex v1.2.0 and on 2.6.6.0 (the hooked instructions
occur once in both executables, and v1.2.0's conditional-jump helper is
byte-identical to v1.3.1's), and a save made before the install. Neither
component has been accepted in real gameplay yet.

# v0.8.6 — Time Stop and Haste effect clocks

New component 360, Time Stop and Haste effect clocks, for BG2:EE and EET with
EEex. It follows Christopher's rule: in a Time Stop everything but the caster
stops, and Haste and Slow change only actions and movement.

- **Time Stop:** the engine freezes creatures in a Time Stop but lets their
  effects run on, so a frozen creature's spells and potions could run out and
  its poison or regeneration count down while it could not act. With 360 the
  frozen creatures' spell and item durations, delayed effects, poison, disease,
  regeneration and repeated effects wait and continue after the stop with the
  time they had left. The caster and Time-Stop-immune creatures keep their
  normal clocks. Clouds and other area effects already wait out a stop and are
  left alone.
- **Haste and Slow** no longer speed up or slow down regeneration, poison,
  disease and repeated effects; these pulse at the normal rate. Actions, attacks
  and movement stay hasted or slowed. Spell Revisions' Haste and Improved Haste
  do not use the engine's Haste and stay as they are; engine Haste from items,
  potions such as Oil of Speed and other mods is corrected. Time Stop is
  corrected with or without Spell Revisions.

360 needs EEex, installed before it, and the 64-bit 2.7.3.0 executable; start
the game through InfinityLoader. The installer reads the executable's header
and refuses every other build, such as 2.6.6.0. Steam's and GOG's 2.7.3.0
executables are the same file; Beamdog's build could not be compared and
installs only if it is that same file. The correction switches itself on at
every game start, without the console, and stays on across loads; saved games
contain nothing of it. Before its first change it checks EEex, the executable
and the eight code locations it changes. If anything is unexpected, the game
runs natively, and one line in InfinityLoader's log says why; there is never a
message in the game.

Install 360 before starting a playthrough: adding or removing it during one is
not supported. Saving during a Time Stop, and dispel, cure or death during one,
are not covered yet. No other component changed.

The package scripts now write the `.sha256` files with LF line endings, so
`sha256sum -c` accepts them on every system. The published files of v0.8.1 to
v0.8.5 end in CRLF and stay as they are; to check one of those, remove the
carriage return first, for example
`tr -d '\r' < FILE.sha256 | sha256sum -c`.

on synthetic games). In the Time

been accepted in real gameplay yet.

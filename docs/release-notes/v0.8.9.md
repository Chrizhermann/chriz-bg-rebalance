# v0.8.9 — Pierce Magic and Spellstrike texts

Pierce Magic (210) and Spellstrike (211) changed what the spells do but described it in
a short text with no header, and their scrolls and wand still described other mechanics.
This release writes the texts the way Spell Revisions writes every other spell and brings
everything that shows the old mechanic up to date. No mechanic changed.

- **The spell text** has Spell Revisions' layout: the name, Level, School, Range, Duration,
  Casting Time, Area of Effect and Saving Throw, the intro, the list of spell protections
  the spell removes, and what 210 / 211 install (Pierce Magic: Magic Resistance reduced by
  half for 5 rounds, by at least 10 and at most 40 points, recasts renew it; Spellstrike:
  15% arcane and divine spell failure for 2 rounds, not stacking). The invisible-target note
  appears as before only when the spell can target invisible creatures.
- **Scrolls and other items** that learn or cast the spell and still show its old description
  (Scroll of Pierce Magic, Scroll of Spellstrike) show the new one. An item with its own text
  keeps it, and the installer prints its name.
- **The Wand of Spell Striking** reaches Pierce Magic through SCS's wand spell. Its Pierce
  Magic charge line ("Lowers target's Magic Resistance by 20% for 2 turns ...") is replaced,
  and nothing else in its text. A wand whose line is not recognizable keeps its text, and
  the installer prints its name.
- **Other spells** that cast the same payload and show the old description show the new
  one (Artisan's Kitpack's monk Spellstrike).
- **Texts-only components 212 (Pierce Magic) and 213 (Spellstrike)** in the `CBR_SR_ANTIMAGIC`
  tail patch do exactly this for an installation that already has the changed mechanic,
  from the main installer, from the 210 / 211 tail or from a private tail, without touching
  the mechanic. They refuse a payload that does not carry it. 213 keeps a Spellstrike text
  that already has the layout and states the 15%.

Requirements are those of 210 and 211. 212 and 213 need BG2:EE or EET and Spell Revisions
main component 0, and no EEex of their own.

Automated checks: 833 tests, OK, 26 skipped (private captures) with WeiDU 249. The new ones run WeiDU on synthetic games: the layout of both
texts, scrolls that share the spell's description strref, carry a copy of its text or have
their own, a scroll whose text another mod changed, a wand with the line present, with
another wording and without it, items with inline Magic Resistance effects, unrelated
and malformed items and spells, a second install, uninstalling, a payload that is the
spell itself, and 212 / 213 over a private tail, over the main installer, without the
mechanic and without Spell Revisions. A dry run of 212 and 213 on copies of the resources of
two real installations (one with a private Spellstrike tail, one a CEBG 0.1.0 test install;
no game folder was written) changed exactly the scroll, wand and spell files described above,
three bytes each. A dry run of 210 on copies of Spell Revisions' own Pierce Magic data did the
same together with the mechanic.

Not tested: a localized game (the texts are English, as before; a wand with another language's
charge line keeps its text and is named), the display in the running game, and a chain of more
than one spell between an item and the payload. Neither component has been accepted in real
gameplay yet.

# v0.8.3 — One potion of each kind at a time; Heroism does not stack

- **Potion uniqueness (330, or 331):** each temporary potion type works once at
  a time on its drinker. Different potion types still combine.
  - Heroism, Power and other potions with a temporary maximum hit point bonus
    keep the first dose until it expires; a second dose is used up without
    effect.
  - Every other temporary potion restarts its normal duration instead of
    stacking its bonus.
  - Healing, cures, permanent and quest effects are unchanged. 331 is the same,
    except that Potions of Genius still stack.
- **Heroism family (332):** Heroism, Greater Heroism (Bardic Wonders), their
  copies and the Potion of Heroism do not combine. The first one active on a
  creature wins; the others have no effect on it until it ends (no refresh, no
  upgrade). Song of Heroism, Aid and the Potion of Power stay separate.

Potions are recognised by what they do, so SCS's copies and potions from other
mods are included. A potion whose second dose cannot be blocked cleanly is named
during installation and left as it is. No EEex is needed and saves are not
edited; effects already active in a save can stack one more time.

No other component changed.

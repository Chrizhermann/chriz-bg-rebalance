# v0.8.1 — More consistent balance options

- **Hardiness:** 40% physical resistance by default recommendation, or 30%.
  SR's extra 20% elemental/magic-damage resistance is a separate option.
- **Pierce Magic:** halves current Magic Resistance, with a 10–40 point
  reduction and zero floor, for five rounds. Recasting refreshes it.
- **Spellstrike:** 15% spell failure for two rounds instead of SR's much
  stronger failure effect.
- **Bracers of Blinding Strike:** use SR's +1 APR Improved Haste, still
  self-only, once per day and lasting 20 seconds.
- **Party physical resistance:** half effectiveness above 80%, capped at 90%.
- **Combat cloud forms:** 75% damage resistance instead of invulnerability.
  Their regeneration remains. Quest protections and vampire death escapes stay.
- **EET elemental arrows:** acid 1d3, cold/fire 1d2, with fire arrows' +2
  attack and physical-damage bonuses removed. Other special arrows stay unchanged.

Each is separately selectable. Hardiness requires SR's Revised Warrior HLAs;
the other SR changes require SR's main component. Pierce Magic and the party
resistance rule require EEex. Install these after their spell/item providers.

Existing components are unchanged. The 95% resistance alternative, Entropy
Shield redesign and Wish Improved Haste change are not part of this release.
The mod does not edit saves or replace already-active saved effects.

Automated installer and runtime-model checks cover behavior, failure rollback,
preservation and uninstall. They do not substitute for an in-engine playtest.

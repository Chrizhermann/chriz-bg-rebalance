# v0.8.5 — Chris Revisions: Aid, Luck, Bless, Chaotic Commands, Prayer and Recitation

New component 340, Chris Revisions: Christopher's own spell balance changes in
one component. The same final values apply with or without Spell Revisions.

- **Aid** grants only hit points: 1d8 +1 per caster level (maximum +10) for one
  turn per level. The +1 to attack rolls, damage and saving throws is gone.
  Casting it again replaces the bonus instead of stacking it. Exact copies,
  such as Bardic Wonders' Troubadour Aid, change with it.
- **Luck** lasts 90 seconds (15 rounds) instead of 5 turns (Spell Revisions) or
  3 rounds (unmodded). Its bonuses are unchanged.
- **Bless** lasts 30 seconds + 1 round per caster level: 36 seconds at level 1,
  90 at level 10 and 150 from level 20, instead of a fixed 5 turns (Spell
  Revisions) or 6 rounds (unmodded). Its bonus is unchanged. Exact copies and
  SCS's instant prebuff copy change with it.
- **Bless and Chaotic Commands for druids:** only Spell Revisions' druid
  exclusion is removed, so druids can cast them again, as in the unmodded game.
  The flag is shared with rangers, who regain Bless too.
- **Prayer and Recitation** (IWD divine spells, for example from IWDification)
  no longer stack. On each creature, Recitation's bonus replaces Prayer's and
  blocks it while it lasts; Recitation's penalty does the same to Prayer's
  penalty. A bonus from one side and a penalty from the other still combine.
  Bless and Chant still stack with everything.

Each part finds its spells through SPELL.IDS and is skipped when they are not
installed. A part that cannot be changed safely is left unchanged with an
installation warning, and the other parts still apply. English descriptions of
the spells and of the scrolls that show them are updated; other wording stays as
it is, with a warning. No other component changed. Install 340 as a new tail
component; the mod does not edit saves. Effects already active in a save keep
their old values until they end, and existing druids should receive Bless and
Chaotic Commands at their next level-up.

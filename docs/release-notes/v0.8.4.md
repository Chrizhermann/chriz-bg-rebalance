# v0.8.4 — Keeps other mods' changes; bracers and Haste do not stack; Divine Protection stays immune

- **Bracers of Blinding Strike (220):** the bracers and SR's Haste or Improved
  Haste no longer combine when the spell is cast first. The bracers' guard now
  sits before the spell's own protection, which made the engine ignore it; a
  repeat run moves a guard that an earlier version left there. Other mods'
  changes to the bracers and to SR's Improved Haste are kept, and the SR copy
  lasts as long as the bracers' own activation (20 seconds on the stock item).
- **Party physical resistance (310):** short immunity windows keep 100%. While
  one timed effect grants full immunity to a damage type on its own, such as
  SCS's Divine Protection and Greater Divine Protection, that type is not
  capped, for exactly as long as the effect lasts. Stacked bonuses, equipment,
  passives and forms stay capped at 90%. Party and enemies now get the same
  Divine Protection, and its description is accurate again.
- **Combat cloud forms (320):** only resistances of 100% or more become 75%.
  Lower values from other mods, including vulnerabilities, stay as they are.
- **Elemental arrows (130):** only each arrow's bow-fired hit changes. Other
  damage, other abilities and other mods' riders keep their effects and their
  description text.
- **Emotion, Courage / Emotion, Hope (301):** other casting times, ranges,
  schools and level headers are accepted, and the descriptions state the
  spell's own values.
- **Cleric of Tempus (400–404):** weapon training, Holy Power and Chaos of
  Battle find their grants, kit-table cells and donor tiers by what they do, so
  Artisan's Kitpack and other layouts work. Other mods' visuals, sounds and
  markers are kept.

These components find what they change by what it does and keep other mods'
changes. Where a change cannot be applied correctly, the installation stops with
the reason instead of guessing. No other component changed. Install as before;
the mod does not edit saves.

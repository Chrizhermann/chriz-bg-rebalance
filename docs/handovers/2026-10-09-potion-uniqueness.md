## Why the draft cannot be delivered

The draft isolates owned runs in private SPLs, uses321 for safe refresh and318
for HP repeat suppression. Offline BG2EE2.7.3.0 / EEex1.3.0 inspection established:

1. Original ITM effects receive caster/dispel level10. Private SPL effects receive
   actor-derived level. Op146's mode2 level argument selects an ability header;
   it does not repair that runtime field. Dispel success changes.
2. Innate helpers lose original mage/priest/song duration scaling. Copying the
   original effect bytes does not preserve the surrounding native delivery.
3. HP guards do not follow the original dispel eligibility. They can outlive a
   removed bonus and reject a legitimate later dose.
4. Guards are checked per incoming effect. A self-source guard can prevent later
   alias guards from being installed, permitting an SCS-equivalent dose to stack.

The initial model checked resource immunity once at spell entry and missed(4).
It was corrected. The resulting failures are retained, not skipped or weakened.
Fixing(3)/(4) alone would not repair(1)/(2), source identity, linked delivery or
reentrant effect lifetime. Moving direct HP into timed177 was separately disproved
by offline native-function assertions: repeated first-use healing is possible.

Precise RVAs, hashes, bounded negative findings and alternative analysis are in
[the diagnosis](../../research/2026-10-09-potion-uniqueness.md). The rejected
[prototype](../../research/prototypes/potion-uniqueness/README.md) is outside the
package tree. A packaging regression enforces that exclusion and the absence of
public330/331. Public TP2 and translations remain at the baseline.
